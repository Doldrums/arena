Connect an experiment

New runs in the monitored folder appear automatically. Hypotheses, lessons, tools and research cells are shown when their records are available.

To watch a different folder, start the monitor with its path:

python -m arena --root /path/to/runs

The monitor reads logs without changing them. Pausing updates stops new data from appearing; the experiment keeps running. Archived runs retain their original status.
