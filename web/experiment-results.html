How to read this board

Canonical evidence comes from the task evaluator. A valid candidate can have a low score; validity does not mean an optimal solution.

Native scores stay attached to their framework and task in each run’s evidence. They are not combined into a global ranking.

Missing measurements stay unknown. Partial evidence and failed processes remain visible, including useful results produced before a failure.
