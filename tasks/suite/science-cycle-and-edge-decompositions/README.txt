# Decompositions of three fixed graphs into few cycles and edges (Erdős-Gallai)

## Task

construct() -> three lists of pieces, one per fixed instance graph generated by instance_graph(n, p, seed) for (20,0.4,11), (25,0.35,12), (30,0.3,13) in the verifier. A piece is a single edge [u, v] or a cycle given by at least three distinct vertices (the closing edge is implied). Pieces must be edge-disjoint and cover all edges. Minimize the total number of pieces. The score counts pieces over three fixed random graphs; it measures decomposition algorithms, not the conjecture's constant.

Source-derived local benchmark; the exact objective and adaptations are specified below.
