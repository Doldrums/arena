# Scientific importance: primary research goal review

A task qualifies when it addresses a substantive scientific or real-world problem and a cited paper's **main objective** is to solve that problem or advance understanding of it. The record must explain why the problem matters and how the local task represents it.

Using a task to evaluate a general agent/framework, or citing only a benchmark, dataset, or tool, is insufficient. A domain-specific AI solution can qualify when the actual scientific problem is its central objective. Mathematics and computational problems can qualify for their contribution to understanding or practical capabilities.

## Examples

- **Methane emission inversion qualifies:** [the Permian Basin methane study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7176423/) aims to quantify actual emissions using satellite observations and atmospheric inversion. The task records differences between its local protocol and that study.
- **Matrix multiplication qualifies:** [the AlphaTensor paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05172-4) aims to discover faster, correct matrix-multiplication algorithms. Its central object is the mathematical/computational problem, and each local task specifies its restricted tensor size and coefficient domain.
- **A2DEPT two-dimensional root finding remains unreviewed:** its five synthetic systems were used in framework evaluation. That citation does not establish a substantive task-specific research objective.
- **Lorenz-96 and the small QAOA/VQE demos remain unreviewed:** a scientific-looking model or a general method paper does not by itself substantiate these local protocols under the main-goal rule.

## Stored evidence and checks

Each supported record in [the evidence registry](../scripts/science_evidence_overrides.json) includes `scientific_problem`, `importance_rationale`, and paper `title`, `url`, `primary_goal`, `task_relation`, and `evidence_role: primary_problem`. The generator sets `criterion: primary_research_goal` and propagates the decision to task/problem manifests, READMEs, the catalog, filters, and statistics.

The schema, registry and statistics reject legacy or incomplete positive claims. The task detail and statistics pages expose the main objective and rationale beside the linked paper. Paper abstracts and task-relevant text were reviewed against local contracts; this does not claim independent reproduction of every paper.
