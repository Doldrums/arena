## Scope and source basis

- `source_verified` means a concrete, pinned native interface was inspected and adapter preparation was checked. It does not establish that its dependencies, credentials, hardware or native evaluation currently work.
- Canonical evaluator bridges currently exist for the documented SCiEL/Shinka profiles. All other recipe evaluations retain native metrics and do not assert shared-task compatibility, held-out validity or comparable cost accounting.
- The primary source inventory is [autoresearch-tasks/catalog/papers.json at 5729caa7](https://github.com/IMRUNya/autoresearch-tasks/blob/5729caa7a3bd4e66c39a447c0d4ee56dc8fa0935/catalog/papers.json). Its task/benchmark source relationships are preserved rather than reinterpreted as engine releases.
- Additional paper-link discovery covered 35 initially unassigned works: 32 arXiv HTML retrievals succeeded; DrSR, ReDAHD and RQGM HTML retrievals failed. Successful retrieval hashes and URLs are retained in the relevant blocker records. A missing implementation in this audit is not proof that none exists.
- EoB, EvoOR-Agent, DrEureka and LaSR were found as genuine source implementations and have concrete native profiles. EvoOR has an explicitly recorded configuration-only portability patch; LaSR uses a separately fingerprinted Julia executable.
- AlphaEvolve's official results repository excludes its search engine. DrSR's CoEvo link is a benchmark source; FunBO's MetaBO link is a comparison/objective source. These links do not supply those papers' search loops.
- READY links an anonymous project that could not be retrieved and pinned in this audit. Community reimplementations, when present, retain their own repository identity and are not attributed to the original paper authors.
