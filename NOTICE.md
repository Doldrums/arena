# Source provenance

The experiment board and observer originate from the private Doldrums/sciel repository. This private repository does not grant a new open-source license to that material.

Task implementations, datasets and evaluator environments remain in their respective repositories and retain their original terms.

Participant entries refer to external repositories. Those declarations do not include or relicense ShinkaEvolve or Darwin Gödel Machine source code.
